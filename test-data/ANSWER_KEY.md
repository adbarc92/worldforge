## Cosmology Contradictions

### 1. Number of Realms
- **Current:** Five Realms (Light, Waves, Images, Echoes, Shadow)
- **v1:** Four Realms (Light, Waves, Images, Shadow — Realm of Echoes is missing)
- **Severity:** Major structural
- **Subtlety:** Moderate — the v1 doc reassigns the Image of Shadow relic's function to the Realm of Shadow instead of a distinct Realm of Echoes

### 2. Number of Eidolons
- **Current:** Nine Eidolons listed
- **v1:** Seven Eidolons listed (missing Truthfulness and Liberation)
- **Severity:** Major
- **Subtlety:** Easy to catch if counting rows

### 3. Shadow Aspects of Eidolons
- **Current:** Humility's shadow is Pride; other shadows are marked "?"
- **v1:** Humility's shadow is Vanity; Wisdom's shadow is Ignorance; Temperance's shadow is Excess; Innovation's shadow is Stagnation
- **Severity:** Medium — Pride vs. Vanity is a meaningful thematic difference
- **Subtlety:** Moderate — the words are related but distinct

### 4. Ascension Ceremony Conditions
- **Current:** Three conditions (Erasure, Consensus, Eclipse)
- **v1:** Four conditions (adds "Sacrifice of the Vessel" — the aspiring god must sacrifice their mortal body)
- **Severity:** Major — changes the mechanics of the climactic event
- **Subtlety:** Low — clearly listed differently

### 5. Succession Timing
- **Current:** Succession "can occur at any time"
- **v1:** Succession "can only occur during a Celestial Eclipse"
- **Severity:** Medium — changes how divine power transfer works
- **Subtlety:** High — easy to skim past

### 6. Exousia Waveform Sex Offset
- **Current:** Male = sine, Female = cosine
- **v1:** Male = cosine, Female = sine (reversed)
- **Severity:** Low (cosmetic) but a clear factual contradiction
- **Subtlety:** High — single-word swap buried in a list

### 7. Amplitude/Frequency Descriptions
- **Current:** Amplitude = intelligence/sapience; Frequency = vitality/energy
- **v1:** Amplitude = "raw power"; Frequency = "endurance"
- **Severity:** Low — similar concepts described differently
- **Subtlety:** High — paraphrasing vs. contradiction is ambiguous

### 8. Number of Magic Forms
- **Current:** Four forms (Primal, Imbuing, Shaping, Blooming)
- **v1:** Three forms (Primal, Imbuing, Shaping — Blooming is missing)
- **Severity:** Major — a whole magic system is absent
- **Subtlety:** Moderate
