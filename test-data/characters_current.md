## Cyrus — The Seeker

A man displaced in both space and time. One of the few surviving Runic Builders — a pre-Disruption profession combining programming and mechanical engineering. Born in the Outskirts to impoverished desert people. Second oldest of five siblings (two brothers, two sisters). Father had unknown talents in Guiding; mother was a Conduit and artisan. Pragmatic, rational, emotionally guarded. Slightly taller than average, muscular, light hair and eyes, tanned skin.

**Arc:** Learning to connect with a new world rather than mourning the old.

## Vaughn — The Hawk

Born to members of an expeditionary company (born on expedition). Became expedition leader at age 18. Hothead and impulsive, but charismatic and deeply loyal. Slightly long face, chiseled brows, deep-set eyes, prominent nose (broken several times), dark brown short hair.

**Arc:** Channeling youthful idealism into real leadership when stakes are highest.

## Phaidros — Second Gnosis

Middle-aged scientist. Close friends with Vaughn's parents before their deaths. Temperamental, hostile toward authority, but masks deep paternal affection with a gruff exterior. His mother pioneered the experiments that transformed Lione.

**Arc:** Confronting his own prejudices; learning that care doesn't require control.

## Sophia — The Torrent

Cyrus's former teammate from pre-Disruption Kyanos. Preserved by the catastrophic event, but awakened two years before Cyrus because she was farther from its center. Hardened by isolation into a cold, cynical assassin working for the Collective. Parents were mid-level Collective members; father was a governor, mother was matron of the arts.

**Arc:** Learning to make her own choices; abandoning dark work to rejoin those who once knew her.

## Adam — The Supreme *(optional recruit)*

Son of a Parliament member and high-ranking Collective official. Product of decades of selective breeding to create the perfect Exousia conduit. Can traverse the Disruption without adverse effects. Near-constant conflict with Vaughn.

**Unconscious desire:** Genuine love and approval.

## Chiranjeevi — The Outsider

Elder of the Vanarite tribe. Arrived from the far north. His initial antagonism with Phaidros gradually transforms into mutual respect. Both men are prideful and reticent.

**Arc:** Deciding whether vengeance or reconciliation better serves his people's future.

## Key Non-Party Figures

- **Kadmos (Adam)** — Early post-Disruption figure who created the first ampoules and developed beam weapons. Not the playable character Adam.
- **Batuo** — Led the Elpidi through the Forest to the Swamp two centuries ago.
- **Dominus** — Leader of the Collective, sealed near Cyrus at the center of the Disruption.
- **Leontios** — Crime boss in Kuklos handling executions and smuggling.
