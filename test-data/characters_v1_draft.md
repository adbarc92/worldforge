## Cyrus — The Seeker

A former Rune Smith from pre-Disruption Kyanos — one of the artisans who inscribed runes onto Devices in the great Ergastiri workshops. Third child of seven siblings in a merchant family. Father was a Wielder of modest skill; mother was a weaver. Quiet and analytical, but with an undercurrent of suppressed rage. Average height, wiry build, dark hair and pale eyes.

He awakens after the Disruption to find the world utterly changed, and his entire family and community lost to time.

**Arc:** Learning to trust and connect with a new world rather than mourning the old.

## Vaughn — The Hawk

Son of a retired military captain and a scholar. Took up adventuring at age 15 when his parents were killed in a Forest expedition. Self-taught leader who earned his company's respect through sheer determination. Lean, tall, with close-cropped red hair and a hawkish face.

**Arc:** Channeling youthful idealism into real leadership when stakes are highest.

## Phaidros — Second Gnosis

A middle-aged professor of natural philosophy at Kuklos's only remaining academy. Colleague of Vaughn's father — the two collaborated on research into Exousia mechanics before the elder's death. Phaidros's wife pioneered the augmentation experiments that eventually led to the creation of the Vdélygma.

**Arc:** Confronting his own prejudices; learning that care doesn't require control.

## Sophia — The Torrent

Cyrus's former partner from the pre-Disruption military division stationed in Kyanos. Like Cyrus, she was preserved, but awakened five years before him because she was farther from the epicenter. Those five years of survival alone in a hostile world have forged her into a ruthless mercenary, selling her skills to whichever faction pays the most — including, at times, the Collective.

Her parents were prominent Collective founders who helped orchestrate the Disruption from within the city government.

**Arc:** Reclaiming her humanity and learning to fight for something other than survival.

## Adam — The Supreme

The Collective's most prized asset. Born from a eugenics program spanning three generations to breed the ultimate Exousia conduit. Adam's father, a Senator in the Union parliament, is one of the Seven Elders. Adam can wield all four forms of magic simultaneously — a feat no other living being can match. He is deployed against the party multiple times before a pivotal moment forces him to choose sides.

**Unconscious desire:** To be seen as more than a weapon.

## Chiranjivi — The Outsider

Chief of the Vanarite people. A warrior in his prime, roughly the same age as Cyrus. Carries the weight of his people's persecution and the memory of the Hill Massacre. His initial hostility toward all outsiders — especially Phaidros, whose wife's research contributed to Vanarite suffering — slowly gives way to grudging respect.

**Arc:** Choosing between vengeance and the harder path of reconciliation.

## Key NPCs

- **Kadmos** — Legendary Runic Builder from the immediate post-Disruption era. Created the first ampoules using pre-Disruption knowledge. Developed energy weapons for the Collective before disappearing.
- **Batuo** — Elpidi prophet who led his people into the Forest 150 years ago to escape Collective persecution.
- **Dominus** — The youngest of the Seven Elders and their de facto leader. Sealed himself at the center of the Disruption alongside Cyrus as insurance against the Ceremony's failure.
- **Leontios** — Former Augmented agent who left the Collective's inner circle to run Kuklos's criminal underworld.
