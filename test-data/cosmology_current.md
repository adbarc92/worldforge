# Cosmology & Metaphysics — Canonical Reference

## The Five Realms

Reality is structured into five layers — three primary and two interstitial.

1. **Realm of Light** — Where the Monad dwells in perfect unity. Symbolized by a burning moon.
2. **Realm of Waves** — Where the Second and the Son reside. Symbolized by a tempest.
3. **Realm of Images** — Where the Eidolons dwell as dualistic deities. Symbolized by a river.
4. **Realm of Echoes** — Accessible via the Image of Shadow relic. Exposure overwhelms and subsumes the mind. Symbolized by concentric circles.
5. **Realm of Shadow** — The foundation of reality where primal forces writhe. Symbolized by a beast.

## Ascension

Two methods exist for a mortal to claim an Eidolon's throne:

### Succession (Voluntary)
An Eidolon chooses a worthy mortal. Power transfers peacefully.

### The Ascension Ceremony (Forced)
Three conditions must be met simultaneously:
1. **Erasure of the Land's Exousia** — Strip and replace the existing Eidolon-aligned Exousia in the land.
2. **Consensus of the Bloodline** — Living members of the targeted Eidolon's bloodline must yield their spiritual claim.
3. **The Celestial Eclipse** — A rare astronomical alignment occurring every several hundred years.

## Exousia

The vital force the Monad imbued into creation. Each soul generates unique waveforms:
- **Sex offset**: Male = sine, Female = cosine
- **Amplitude**: Intelligence/sapience — higher = greater magical potency
- **Frequency**: Vitality/energy — higher = reduced casting costs
- **Eidolon-alignment**: Inherited trait bound to bloodline

### Four Forms of Magic
1. **Primal** — Elemental forces (fire, lightning, etc.)
2. **Imbuing** — Embedding soul fragments into matter to create magical items. Each use costs part of oneself.
3. **Shaping (Vanarism)** — Reshaping body and matter at will.
4. **Blooming** — Proliferating life through Exousia.

### Toxic Exousia
Extracted by force. Corrupts the user's waveform over time. Signs: erratic magic, physical deterioration, spiritual instability.
