# Cosmology — Early Design Notes (v1)

## The Four Realms

Reality is divided into four planes of existence:

1. **Realm of Light** — The Monad's domain. All creation extends from here. Symbol: a burning moon.
2. **Realm of Waves** — Home of the Second and the Son, the first emanations. Symbol: a tempest.
3. **Realm of Images** — Where the Eidolons govern the relationship between divine and mortal. Symbol: a river.
4. **Realm of Shadow** — The deepest layer where primal forces exist in darkness. Symbol: a beast.

The Image of Shadow relic can bridge the Realm of Shadow and the material world. Extended exposure to the Shadow overwhelms the mind — some become purely cognitive entities, others are reduced to bestial nature.

## Ascension

Two paths exist for a mortal to replace an Eidolon:

### Succession
The Eidolon chooses a worthy mortal. Can only occur during a Celestial Eclipse.

### The Ascension Ceremony
Four conditions must be met:
1. **Erasure of the Land's Exousia** — The land's Eidolon-aligned Exousia must be stripped and replaced.
2. **Consensus of the Bloodline** — The targeted Eidolon's bloodline must yield their spiritual claim.
3. **Sacrifice of the Vessel** — The aspiring god must sacrifice their mortal body in a ritual of transformation.
4. **The Celestial Eclipse** — The rare alignment that thins the barriers between realms.

## Exousia

The vital force of creation. Every soul generates Exousia in a unique waveform:
- **Sex offset**: Male = cosine, Female = sine
- **Amplitude**: Raw power — higher amplitude means more potent magic
- **Frequency**: Endurance — higher frequency reduces fatigue from casting
- **Eidolon-alignment**: Inherited through bloodline

### Three Forms of Magic
1. **Primal** — Direct elemental channeling (fire, ice, lightning, earth).
2. **Imbuing** — Fusing soul energy into objects to create magical constructs.
3. **Shaping** — Altering the physical composition of body and matter.

Toxic Exousia — drawn from tainted sources or extracted by violence — offers a shortcut to power. Prolonged use leads to waveform corruption: unstable magic, physical decay, and eventual madness.
