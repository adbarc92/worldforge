# Factions — Canonical Reference

## The Collective

A shadowy conglomerate of political leaders and social figures pursuing the Ascension Ceremony. Analogous to a real-world Illuminati.

### The Sophronoi
The Collective is fundamentally a Sophronoi organization. All Seven Elders are Sophronoi — a distinct race bound to the Eidolon of Humility (shadow: Pride). The Sophronoi originally hail from Sophron, a bountiful southern land.

### Ideology
- **Public:** Henosis — unification of all humanity into one transcendent being.
- **Truth:** Only one soul can ascend. Each of the Seven Elders secretly plans to betray the others.

The Collective deliberately keeps the populace trapped and in despair. Concession is a necessary condition for the Ascension Ceremony's second requirement (Consensus of the Bloodline).

### The Augmented
Humans with Devices fused into their bodies. Biomechanical hybrids serving as elite enforcers. Only the Collective has the technology to make this work.

### The Legacy
Splinter group of reformed Collective agents who broke away.

## The Vanarites

A distinct race (not Glyphein) settled in the Valleys. Called "Outsiders" by Kuklos residents; they call city-dwellers "Dwellers."

### Identity
Able to alter their body's composition at will — Vanarism (also called Shaping). Tied to the Eidolon of Temperance. Before the Disruption, this was a painful process called Gandharvism; the Disruption amplified it dramatically.

Can traverse the Forest with few side effects because their Eidolon-alignment mitigates excess growth.

### History
- Originally from **Gandharva**, a far-northern country. White hair, black skin (like polar bears).
- ~1,000 years ago: Discovered the Giant's Teeth mountains north of Kyanos.
- A tribal schism sent a group called the **Giri** south to the mountains.
- ~200 years pre-Disruption: Giri develop relations with Kyanosites.
- ~100 years pre-Disruption: Giri proliferate, settle the Hills.
- Year 0: Disruption occurs. They rename themselves Vanarites.
- Year 23: Collective begins anti-Vanarite propaganda (accusing them of kidnapping actually done by the Collective).
- Year 28: Adventurer raid kills 30% of their population.

### Society
Governed by a parliament of Elders with a Prime and Sub-Minister. Three internal factions: run, hide, fight. Majority are pacifistic.

Dying Vanarites venture to the Skeletal Tree Yard to fight. These are called **Vetala**.

**The Seed:** A massive crystalline structure at the valley's heart, with a cave network containing storehouses of knowledge on vellum.

## The Elpidi / Order of Pious Sun

A distinct race wielding Blooming magic. Eidolon: Hope (shadow: Despair).

### History
Originally possessed knowledge of the Sacred Son. Worshipped his piety through the Eidolon of Wisdom. Over time, this became corrupted into sun-worship.

Two centuries ago, led by **Batuo**, they fled Collective persecution through the Forest to the Swamp, settling a hidden village accessible through a door in giant tree roots. They later fortified with rock provided by the Vanarites in exchange for martial and spiritual teachings.

Their protection from Forest madness: they travel in sufficiently large groups to mitigate its effects.

## The Glyphein

The dominant race of Kyanos/Kuklos, subdivided through selective breeding:
- **Wielders** — Bred for magical precision and control.
- **Conduits** — Bred for high Exousia throughput; magic manifests involuntarily; low fertility.

30 years before the story: Conduits orchestrated a mass suicide in protest of enslavement, crippling Wielder infrastructure. ~20 survivors leveraged newfound influence into oligarchic power.

## The Union

The standing government of Kuklos Anankes. Maintains a facade of legitimate authority but is constrained by the Collective's shadow influence.
