# Factions — Working Draft (v1)

## The Collective

The primary antagonist faction. A secret society controlling Kuklos from the shadows, working to complete an Ascension Ceremony.

### Composition
The Collective's leadership — the Council of Five — are all Sophronoi, a race tied to the Eidolon of Humility. The Sophronoi come from the eastern coastal territory of Sophron, a maritime trading power. Rank-and-file membership is drawn from all races, though Sophronoi hold every position of real authority.

### Ideology
They preach a doctrine of henosis: collective transcendence into a unified godlike entity. In truth, the Ascension can only elevate one soul — each Council member plans to be the one who claims the throne.

The populace is deliberately kept in a state of hopelessness. This despair feeds the second condition of Ascension.

### Sub-Groups

**The Augmented:** Soldiers who have undergone Device-fusion to become living weapons. The process requires a Conduit to bind the Device to the host's Exousia waveform — the Collective maintains a small number of captive Conduits for this purpose.

**The Legacy:** Former Collective operatives who defected. Led by a rogue Elder named Maren who discovered the truth about the Ascension's one-soul limit and was disgusted.

## The Vanarites

An outsider race dwelling in the mountains beyond the Forest. Not Glyphein — they are a genetically distinct people with innate Shaping magic tied to the Eidolon of Temperance.

### Origins
The Vanarites hail from the northern territory of **Vanaheim**, a snow-covered highland. Their natural adaptation — white hair, dark skin — evolved for the extreme northern climate.

Approximately 500 years ago, a splinter faction called the **Giri** migrated south and discovered the mountains near Kyanos. Relations with the city were initially hostile but warmed over the next century as trade developed.

### Post-Disruption
The Disruption massively amplified their Shaping ability — what was once painful and slow became fluid and instinctive. The Giri renamed themselves "Vanarites" in honor of this transformation.

The Collective targeted them early, using propaganda to frame them as kidnappers. In Year 28, a state-sponsored raid killed nearly half the Vanarite population in the Hills, forcing survivors deeper into the mountains. Those near death undertake a final pilgrimage to the Skeletal Tree Yard to die fighting — these warriors are called **Vetala**.

### Society
A council of Elders governs. Three factions: those who want to flee, those who want to hide, and those who want war. The war faction has been growing since the Hill Massacre.

## The Elpidi / Order of Pious Sun

Practitioners of Blooming magic — the ability to proliferate and accelerate life. Bound to the Eidolon of Hope.

Originally a sect within Kyanos's broader religious landscape, devoted to the Sacred Son. They were driven from the city 150 years ago when the Collective scapegoated them after a particularly devastating expansion of the Forest. Their leader, a monk named **Batuo**, guided them through the Forest to a hidden enclave in the Swamp.

The Elpidi are not a separate race — they are a Glyphein bloodline whose Exousia naturally resonates with Blooming. Their monastic tradition, influenced by their Tibetan and Shaolin cultural roots, emphasizes communal living. Critically, their communal travel practice — always moving in large groups — is what protects them from Forest Sickness, though they attribute it to spiritual purity.

## The Glyphein

Kyanos's dominant population, divided by selective breeding into specialized castes:

- **Wielders** — Bred for active magical control.
- **Conduits** — Bred for raw Exousia throughput. Magic manifests involuntarily.
- **Melders** — Extremely rare individuals who combine both Wielder and Conduit traits.

Fifty years before the story, the Conduits — enslaved as living batteries for Device technology — orchestrated a coordinated mass suicide. Over 90% perished. The few dozen survivors leveraged their sudden scarcity into political power, establishing themselves as an oligarchic class.
