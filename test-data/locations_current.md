# Locations — Canonical Reference

## Kyanos

*Literally: "Lapis Lazuli"*

The pre-Disruption name for Kuklos Anankes. A desert city built beside a river flowing from the Giant's Teeth mountains. At its peak, home to hundreds of thousands — the largest city in the known world. Founded by nomadic desert warriors around a church dedicated to the Eidolon of Wisdom.

Construction: primarily limestone. Economy driven by Device development and Exousia engineering. Notable feature: the Ergastiri (workshops) where Runic Builders operated.

## Kuklos Anankes (Kuklos)

*Literally: "City of Fate"*

The main setting. After the Disruption, Kyanos was renamed Kuklos Anankes. Surrounded by forest on all sides, cut off from the outside world. Civilization reduced to near-agrarian levels.

### Eight Districts (Greek names)
- Nous (Mind), Elpis (Hope), Zoe (Life), Logos (Word)
- Ennoia (Silence), Synesis (Intelligence), Sophia (Wisdom)
- Eighth district unnamed

Many districts suffered structural damage and are deemed uninhabitable. An exousia-inert zone protects the city; the Collective plans to disable it when terraforming completes.

Devices are stigmatized as demonic (Collective propaganda). Walls along the forest border need rebuilding approximately every two centuries due to forest encroachment.

## The Unknown — Layered Zones

The region beyond Kuklos. Each layer is progressively more transformed by the Disruption's metaphysical terraforming.

1. **The Rainforest/Jungle** — Ancient trees, unstable Exousia, intelligent undergrowth.
2. **The Marshes/Swamp** — Floating islands of moss and bone. Home to the Elpidi village.
3. **The Caustic Rise** — Zone of slow, poisonous death. Toxins seep through wounds.
4. **The Crest (Graves)** — Flat barren plains used for burial. Disciples of the Circle place stone rings.
5. **The Infinite River** — Currents run in impossible directions. Corpse Island.
6. **The Stonefield** — Petrified forest. Jagged spikes, massive stone redwoods.
7. **The Mountains** — Noble Retreat, Vanarist Proving Ground.
8. **The Valleys** — Contains Vanar (Vanarite settlement), The Seed, forges.
9. **The Skeletal Tree Yard** — Bleached trees, graveyard of the old world. Where Vetala go to die fighting.
10. **The Desert of Mist** — Sand and fog. Few return.
11. **Disruption's Shell (Void)** — Idyllic meadow. Anyone passing through the fog vanishes.
