# Story & Narrative — Canonical Reference

## Premise

Cyrus awakens centuries after the Disruption — a deliberately engineered Ascension Ceremony that froze time at the heart of Kyanos, transformed the surrounding desert into layered zones of alien reality, and killed most of the population.

The world he wakes into is Kuklos Anankes, trapped inside a vast forest. The Collective — seven Sophronoi Elders — controls the city, preaching collective transcendence while each secretly competing to claim an Eidolon's throne. The populace is kept in despair by design.

Cyrus forms a company with Vaughn, Phaidros, and others to salvage lost Devices, uncover truth, and confront the Collective before the Ceremony's climax.

**Central question:** Should anyone be allowed to ascend?

## The Disruption

The Disruption is a combination of three engineered events:
1. Metaphysical terraforming via Devices — stripping and replacing the land's Exousia.
2. Temporal preservation — a Device at Kyanos's center froze time, preserving Collective members.
3. An exousia-inert zone maintained around the city as protection.

The Ceremony has not yet reached its climax. The Collective plans to disable the inert zone when outer terraforming completes.

## Endings

Five endings based on player choice:

1. **Walk Away** — Cyrus departs through the Fog.
2. **Confront** — Standard ending. Party storms the Citadel.
3. **Madness** — Cyrus succumbs to Forest corruption.
4. **Concession** — Cyrus yields to the Collective. If passive, Dominus ascends and save is deleted.
5. **Secret: Ascension** — If Cyrus knows the Ascension conditions and draws his weapon during the Ceremony, he can claim the throne himself, becoming the Eidolon of Innovation.

## Narrative Themes

- **The Eternal Spiral** — Emergence, Manifestation, Transformation cycles.
- **Balance** — Comfort vs. adventure, order vs. meaningful chaos.
- **Trust and Shadow** — Trust tested by the necessity of integrating one's own darkness.
- **Free Will** — Every choice matters in a world of Ascension and toxic Exousia.
- **Circles and Cycles** — Time as spiral; the motif of the circle everywhere.

## Key Plot Points

- The Elpidi were driven from Kuklos two centuries ago by Collective persecution.
- The Conduits' mass suicide 30 years before the story crippled the Glyphein power structure.
- Sophia awakened 2 years before Cyrus; those years of isolation hardened her.
- The Celestial Eclipse occurs every several hundred years.
- Kadmos (Adam) — a historical figure, not the party member — created ampoules in the early post-Disruption era.
