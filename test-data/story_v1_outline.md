# Story Outline — Early Draft (v1)

## Premise

Cyrus awakens in Kuklos Anankes (formerly Kyanos) centuries after the Disruption — a catastrophe that transformed the surrounding desert into a vast forest of layered reality zones. The Collective, led by a Council of Five Sophronoi elders, controls the city from the shadows. They preach collective transcendence but secretly compete to claim the throne of the Eidolon of Wisdom for themselves.

Cyrus joins Vaughn, Phaidros, and a growing company of allies to uncover the truth and stop the Ascension before the next Celestial Eclipse — which occurs every thousand years and is now imminent.

## Act Structure

### Act 1: Awakening
Cyrus wakes in the sealed chamber at the center of the Disruption. He escapes into Kuklos and is taken in by Vaughn's expedition company. Phaidros, a professor and old friend of Vaughn's father, provides context: the world has been like this for centuries, and the Collective rules through fear and scarcity.

The party undertakes their first Forest expedition — an ampoule recharge run that goes wrong, drawing them deeper than intended.

### Act 2: The Unknown
The party encounters Paidi's monastic order in the Swamp. They learn the Elpidi were driven from the city six generations ago. The monks' village is attacked by Collective forces, forcing the party deeper into the Unknown.

Along the way they recruit:
- **Lione** — rescued from a Collective research facility in the Caustic Rise
- **Euphen** — encountered wandering the Stonefield, seemingly immune to Forest Sickness
- **Chiranjeevi** — chief of the Vanarites, initially hostile

The party discovers that the Disruption is not a natural disaster but an engineered Ascension Ceremony. The "metaphysical terraforming" is stripping the land of its existing Exousia signature to meet the first condition of Ascension.

### Act 3: Convergence
Sophia appears — Cyrus's former partner, now a Collective assassin. She's been tracking the party on Dominus's orders. A confrontation leads to her defection.

Adam, the Collective's bred weapon, is deployed against the party. He can be recruited if Cyrus makes specific choices; otherwise he remains an antagonist.

The party learns the Celestial Eclipse is days away. They must reach the four towers at the cardinal points of the Mistwall and retrieve the keys that can disrupt the Ceremony.

### Act 4: Endgame
Return to Kuklos. Final confrontation with the Collective leadership. Cyrus faces the central question: should anyone be allowed to ascend?

## Endings

Four endings based on player choice:

1. **Walk Away** — Cyrus departs through the Fog, leaving Kuklos to its fate.
2. **Confront** — Cyrus and the party storm the Citadel and stop the Ceremony by force.
3. **Madness** — Cyrus succumbs to Forest corruption (triggered by excessive Toxic Exousia use).
4. **Concession** — Cyrus yields to the Collective. If he fights his former allies, the Collective wins. If he remains passive, Dominus ascends.
