# Technology & Devices — Design Notes (v1)

## Devices

Objects that harness Exousia through runic inscription. A Device has five essential components:

1. **Core (Germ)**: A container of refined exousium crystal that stores raw Exousia.
2. **Spark (Source)**: A charge of pure Exousia, imparted by a Wielder during the forging process.
3. **Matrix (Set)**: The runic inscription pattern that governs power flow.
4. **Shell (Form)**: The physical housing.
5. **Anchor**: A binding element that ties the Device to its wielder's Exousia waveform, preventing use by others.

Without all five components, a Device is inert.

### Named Devices

**Weapons:**
- **Asura** (Assurance): A legendary black blade forged to fight magical creatures. Distorts the air around it in a magic-consuming vortex. Created in the capital city by a military smith as a countermeasure against rogue Wielders.
- **Abe** (Aberration/Ability): A gauntlet that absorbs and redirects kinetic energy.

**Objects:**
- Skin of the Chosen, Altesh the Reaver, Wreth the Luce, and others.

## Ampoules

Portable Exousia batteries developed by the Collective's research division in the decades following the Disruption. They solved the critical problem of Device operation inside the exousia-inert zone around Kuklos. Ampoules are recharged by exposure to ambient Exousia in the Forest.

The Collective initially controlled the ampoule supply, using it as leverage over the populace. The technology eventually leaked to the public, but the Collective retains the most efficient charging methods.

## Runic Builders (Rune Smiths)

Pre-Disruption artisans who inscribed runes onto Devices. Rune Smiths worked in specialized workshops called Ergastiri, crafting both the runic patterns and the physical forms of Devices. They were a respected craftsman class, though subordinate to the Wielders who provided the Spark.

Cyrus was a Rune Smith — one of the last practitioners of this dying art. His knowledge of runic inscription gives him a unique ability to repair and modify salvaged Devices.

## Technology Tiers

**Public (Kuklos):**
- Primitive metalwork (swords, bows, crossbows)
- Basic agriculture and textiles
- Devices feared and stigmatized

**Adventurers:**
- Railguns (ampoule-powered)
- Salvaged Devices (unreliable)

**Collective (secret):**
- Gunpowder firearms (200 years of covert development)
- Energy weapons (Kadmos's designs)
- Biomechanical augmentation
- Adaptive armor, cloaking, dropships

**Black Market:**
- Bubble shots, energy cannons
- Wresters (life-force drains)
- Sparktakers (Device energy theft)
